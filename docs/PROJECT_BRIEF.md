# Project Brief — SoSparkDesigns (2026–2027)

## 1. Executive Summary & Identity
- **Entity**: SoSparkDesigns (also operating as SoSpark Design).
- **Core Role**: Creative digital agency and parent company.
- **Key Brand Relationships**:
  - **Subz Empire**: Digital asset and subscription ecosystem (`https://subzempire.com`).
  - **SparkForge** (Sparkfroge): Flagship email verification and deliverability engine (`https://sparkfroge.pro.bd`).
- **Leadership**: Directed by Soahim Rahman Tasin (`soahimrahman2024@gmail.com`).
- **Primary Mission**: Rebuild the official agency and parent-company web platform from the ground up to establish commercial authority, showcase genuine agency capabilities and owned digital ventures, and convert qualified clients without relying on fake claims or generic AI templates.

## 2. Business Goals & Value Proposition
1. **Commercial Credibility**: Present SoSparkDesigns as an authentic, high-caliber creative agency that delivers real client work across branding, web design, Next.js engineering, motion, and digital growth.
2. **Parent Company Positioning**: Clearly represent SoSparkDesigns as both a service agency and an incubator/parent company actively developing flagship products (Subz Empire, SparkForge).
3. **Qualified Inquiries**: Drive prospective clients through a friction-free, validated project quotation and inquiry funnel.
4. **Zero Fabrication**: Cease all fabricated testimonials, synthetic client logos, and unverified statistical metrics. Every claim must be truthful or explicitly labeled as a demonstrative fixture during development.

## 3. Target Audiences
- **Founders & Seed/Series A Startups**: Requiring brand identity, MVP web application design, and modern Next.js implementation.
- **SMEs & Growing Enterprises**: Upgrading legacy websites to high-speed, SEO-dominating digital experiences.
- **Ventures & Partners**: Exploring collaboration or integration with SoSparkDesigns' owned digital products (Subz Empire, SparkForge).

## 4. Core Service Catalog
1. **Brand Identity & Systems**: Logo suites, typographic hierarchies, color theory, brand architecture, and master design guidelines.
2. **UI/UX & Web Design**: Editorial-precision Figma prototypes, accessible user journeys, and component systems.
3. **Full-Stack Web Development**: Next.js App Router, TypeScript, performant serverless endpoints, and sub-second load times.
4. **Graphic & Marketing Collateral**: Pitch decks, luxury packaging, editorial publications, and high-impact vector artwork.
5. **Video & Motion Graphics**: Cinematic brand reels, kinetic motion, social ad creative, and product animations.
6. **Page & Growth Management**: Retainer-based content curation, asset scheduling, and engagement monitoring.

## 5. Constraints & Infrastructure Boundaries
- **Primary Color**: Blue (`#2563EB`). **Strict Rule**: Green/neon lime is forbidden as a primary accent.
- **Hosting / Budget**: Free hosting tiers and existing infrastructure (Cloudflare, Vercel Free, Render Free, GitHub).
- **Commercial Restrictions**: Evaluate Vercel Hobby commercial limitations; avoid vendor lock-in that risks sudden takedown or fee cliffs.
- **Backend Architecture**: Prefer native Next.js Server Components, Server Actions, and Route Handlers over maintaining heavy separate backend microservices unless explicitly justified.
